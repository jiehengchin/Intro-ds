**Project Objective:**

This group project focuses on **data thinking**: formulating questions,
designing data collection methods, understanding data ethics, and visual
storytelling. Your task is to identify a valuable real-world problem and
apply Data Science principles to analyse it.\
\
**Rules for this project:**

1.  **Solving a real life problem**: bigger problem means bigger impact,
    and bigger value

2.  **Programming not essential:** All analysis and visualization could
    be done using no-code tools like Excel, Google Sheets, Canva, or
    Tableau Public.

3.  **Math Derivations not essential:** Focus on descriptive statistics
    (e.g. mean, median, standard deviation, correlation) and visual
    insights (e.g. box plots, scatter plots).

**Data Source Requirements (Choose One):**

- **Primary Collection:** Design a survey or conduct field observations
  to collect a minimum of 100 data points.

- **Literature Extraction:** Extract, compile, and standardize summary
  statistics from at least 8 academic papers or official industry
  reports.

**AI & Collaboration Policy:**

While you may use AI (e.g. Google Gemini, ChatGPT) to check grammar or
brainstorm ideas, **the core intellectual work must be your own.** Your
data must be hyper-local or cited its origin. During the Q&A of your
presentation, you will be asked specific questions about your
methodology. Inability to explain your group's reasoning will result in
severe penalties.

**Deliverable 1: The Report (Min 8 Pages) - 40%**

- **Abstract & Introduction:** Define the problem and why it matters.

- **Methodology:** Detail exactly how you collected your data. If you
  used a survey, what were the questions? How did you avoid bias?

- **Analysis & Visualization:** Include at least 4 distinct, clearly
  labelled charts (e.g., bar charts for comparisons, scatter plots for
  relationships).

- **Conclusion & Limitations:** What did you learn? What were the flaws
  in your data? each members’ responsibility

### Group Project Options (please choose one for each group)

#### Option 1: Propose Your Data Solution

- **Concept:** Groups must identify a local, observable problem within
  the CityU or neighbourhood community and collect their own primary
  data through surveys or physical observation.

- **Example Topics:** Canteen wait times vs. class schedules;
  lift(elevator) waiting time vs. student traffic; student study hours
  vs. mental health self-assessments.

- **Tips:**\
  - Define your hypothetical business and the problem\
  - Students shall collaborate to design a structured unbiased survey
  questionnaire, distribute and collect the feedback (minimum 100
  responses)
